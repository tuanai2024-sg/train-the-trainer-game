Easy-read version for beginners. Vertex Group is a simulated company; every number is illustrative.

150 everyday jobs in a large group, redone by an optimizer

Each card is a job people currently do from experience: loading trucks, ordering stock, planning shifts, setting prices. The optimizer tries a very large number of ways and keeps the best one for that job's goal and limits. The card shows how it goes today, what the optimizer achieves, what it is worth, and which ideas to know first. Unfamiliar term? Open the finance primer.

  1. 1. TodayHow the job is done now, measured on sample data.
  2. 2. The optimizerOn the same data, how much better the optimizer does.
  3. 3. What to doApply now, run a 90-day pilot, or defer.

The yearly amount is not the gap shown on the card: the improvement on sample data is scaled up to the full scope (every warehouse, route or store) and multiplied by a factor for the share kept in real operations. For cash-release problems the cash arrives once; the yearly value is the carrying cost saved, 14% of that cash.
